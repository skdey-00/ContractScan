RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into on January 1, 2026, by and between Rajesh Kumar ("Landlord") and Priya Sharma ("Tenant").

1. PROPERTY: Landlord hereby leases to Tenant the residential property located at 42 MG Road, Flat 3C, Koramangala, Bangalore, Karnataka 560034 ("Premises").

2. TERM: The lease term shall be for a period of 11 months, commencing on February 1, 2026, and ending on December 31, 2026. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either party provides written notice of termination at least 30 days prior to the end of the current term.

3. RENT: Tenant shall pay monthly rent of Rs. 28,000, due on the 1st day of each calendar month. Rent shall be payable via bank transfer to Landlord's designated account. A late fee of Rs. 500 per day shall apply to any rent payment received after the 5th day of the month.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT: Tenant shall deposit Rs. 1,68,000 (six months' rent) as security deposit upon signing this Agreement. The deposit shall be refunded within 60 days after the termination of the lease, less any deductions for damages beyond normal wear and tear, unpaid rent, or other charges. No interest shall be paid on the security deposit.

5. RENT ESCALATION: Landlord reserves the right to increase the monthly rent by up to 20% per year with only 7 days written notice to Tenant. Tenant shall have no right to dispute such increase and acceptance of the increase shall be a condition of continued tenancy.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS: Tenant shall be responsible for all maintenance and repairs to the Premises, including but not limited to plumbing, electrical, appliance repairs, structural repairs, pest control, and painting, at Tenant's sole cost and expense. Landlord shall not be liable for any condition of the Premises whatsoever.

7. USE OF PREMISES: The Premises shall be used exclusively for residential purposes. No part of the Premises shall be used for any business, commercial, or illegal purpose. Tenant shall not sublet or assign this lease without prior written consent of Landlord. No pets are allowed without written permission.

8. TERMINATION: Landlord may terminate this Agreement at any time with 15 days written notice for any reason or no reason. Tenant may not terminate this Agreement before the end of the lease term. Early termination by Tenant shall result in forfeiture of the entire security deposit.

9. DEFAULT: If Tenant fails to pay rent within 10 days of the due date, Landlord may immediately re-enter and take possession of the Premises without any court order or legal process. All of Tenant's belongings may be removed and disposed of at Landlord's discretion without any liability to Landlord.

10. INDEMNIFICATION: Tenant agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Landlord from any and all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, and expenses (including attorney's fees) arising from Tenant's use of the Premises, including claims by Tenant's guests, invitees, and family members, and including claims arising from Landlord's negligence.

11. GOVERNING LAW: This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Karnataka. Any disputes shall be resolved exclusively in courts located in Bangalore. Tenant waives any right to trial by jury.

12. ACCESS: Landlord or Landlord's agents may enter the Premises at any time without prior notice or Tenant's consent for inspection, repairs, to show the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers, or for any other purpose Landlord deems necessary.

13. QUIET ENJOYMENT: Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting Tenant any right to quiet enjoyment of the Premises. Landlord reserves the right to conduct construction, renovation, or any other activities in or around the Premises at any time.

14. UTILITIES: Tenant shall be responsible for payment of all utilities including electricity, water, gas, internet, and any other services. Landlord shall not be responsible for any interruption in utility services.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first written above.
